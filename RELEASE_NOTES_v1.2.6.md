# Visualiser Studio v1.2.6

## Surprise me

Find a fresh look in one click. Open the Style menu in Create and press **Surprise me** to pick a different visualiser style with a coordinated, atmospheric colour palette.

- Five palettes: Northern Lights, Peach Dusk, Moon Garden, Velvet Night and Coastal Blue.
- Works in both Create layouts, with multicolour palettes for Complex styles when Advanced mode is enabled.
- Undo the whole change with Ctrl+Z, or keep it in your saved project or template.
- Keeps your soundtrack, MIDI attachment, automation and export settings.

## Test the updater

If you have v1.2.5 installed, open **Settings → Updates → Check for updates**. Once v1.2.6 finishes downloading, choose **Restart to install**. Save any unsaved work when prompted, then check that Settings shows v1.2.6.

For a manual installation, download **Visualiser-Studio-Setup-1.2.6.exe**. The blockmap and latest.yml assets are for automatic updates.
