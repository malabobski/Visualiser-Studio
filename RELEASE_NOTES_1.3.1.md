# Visualiser Studio 1.3.1

## Your people, closer
- Direct messages for friends, with unread badges and clickable message notifications.
- Unread messages expire after 30 days. Opened messages are saved in encrypted local history before being removed from the delivery queue. History is specific to that computer or browser.
- Optional activity sharing in My Corner lets friends see what you are doing or listening to.
- Open someone's corner from their name or profile icon and manage your friendship there.
- Announcements have their own feed rail, with mobile announcements above the main feed.
- Creator tools support pinned posts, announcements, post editing/deletion, account verification and private notifications. Role badges appear beside display names.
- Four new social achievements: Better together, A voice in the crowd, Pass the colours and Just between us.

## A cleaner workspace
- Shared looks use their saved template names. Saving a template now asks for a name.
- Delete saved projects with confirmation; deleted projects move to Trash or Recycle Bin without deleting the original source audio.
- Right-click projects and templates for their common actions.
- More secondary-colour accents, a cleaner desktop-audio button and four main Create actions.
- Improved mini-player text alignment and settings, including compact-window layout and outside-click dismissal.
- Settings sections open one at a time, and tooltips share the Create stage's styling across the app and mini player.
- Removed the extra Back buttons from Templates, Batch Export and Live.

## Notifications and shortcuts
- Notifications now animate into the top-right corner, clear of Quick Actions, and play a soft sound. Achievements have their own sound.
- Automatic update downloads show an update-ready notification. Restarting still asks first and waits for a completed save.
- Enter sends a DM; Shift+Enter starts a new line. Ctrl+Enter posts to Community. Buttons show the shortcut on hover.
- Alt+1–5 switches sidebar tabs in order; Alt+6 opens Miniplayer; Ctrl+, opens Settings. On Mac, use Option and Command respectively.

## Downloads
- Windows: `Visualiser-Studio-Setup-1.3.1.exe`.
- Apple Silicon: `Visualiser-Studio-1.3.1-mac-arm64.dmg`.
- Intel Mac: `Visualiser-Studio-1.3.1-mac-x64.dmg`.
- Mac requires macOS 14.2 or newer. Builds are ad-hoc signed and are not Apple-notarized; first launch may require **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
- Windows automatic updates use the Windows installer only. Mac updates remain manual; no Mac automatic-update feed is published.
- The companion website has a dedicated Community workspace with friends, messages and announcements. Its HTML is uploaded separately from app releases.

Feed posts are visible to signed-in Community members; the Friends filter changes the view, not a post's visibility. Shared templates exclude audio, MIDI, automation, image layers and local file paths. Messages use encrypted local storage after delivery, rather than end-to-end encryption.
