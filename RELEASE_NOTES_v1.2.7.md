# Visualiser Studio v1.2.7

## A smoother place to hang out with your music

- A branded installer with a dark purple welcome, clearer install progress and a launch option. In-app updates skip the setup screens.
- Achievements in Settings → Help & guidance: collect all 60 diamond messages and discover 22 secrets, with saved progress and subtle hints.
- Click an achievement notification to jump to its card. Notifications appear only for new discoveries; Reset progress lets you start again.
- Secret text triggers now work in project names and Quick Actions too.
- The Live fullscreen player starts as cover art. Click for controls, drag the cover to move it, and click away to collapse. Settings include cover size and fullscreen visibility.
- Roomier mini-player settings that stay on screen, close when clicking blank space and avoid accidental resizing.
- Fixed the repeated cover-art blur when closing and prevented native image dragging from stranding player controls.
- Interface text no longer highlights. Tab keeps its view-switch shortcut without moving focus or repeating while held.
- Discord’s website button and cover-art link now open the Visualiser Studio website.

## Updates

This release moves future updates to our public releases repository. Existing v1.2.5 and v1.2.6 installations can receive v1.2.7 through Settings → Updates without a separate download. Your projects, preferences and achievements stay in the same app profile.

For a manual installation, use Visualiser-Studio-Setup-1.2.7.exe. The blockmap and latest.yml files are for the updater.
