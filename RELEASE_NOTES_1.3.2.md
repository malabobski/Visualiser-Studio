# Visualiser Studio 1.3.2

More ways to hang out with your music.

## Fresh looks
- Meet **Laser Bloom**, **Spectral Wings** and **Glass Comets**: glowing ribbons, moving light and independent comet trails that react to your music.
- Turning Movement off returns the visualiser to its unrotated position.
- Shared looks that need a newer app now explain that an update is required, instead of saving as the wrong style.

## Easier to get started
- A new first-launch introduction reveals the basics one step at a time, with smooth transitions and a Skip option.
- Stage Designer, MIDI and Automation have short introductions the first time you open them. Replay them from Help & guidance.
- Achievements are more compact, with animated hints inside their cards.

## Your workspace, tidied up
- A themed file picker replaces the desktop file dialogs, with drive shortcuts, folder search, a parent-folder entry and animated image previews.
- Drag Stage Designer layers to change their order, including above or below the visualiser.
- Click the Automation timeline to move the playhead.
- Quick Actions are grouped into Projects, Design & export, Playback & live audio, Go to and App.
- Move between the open File and Edit menus without clicking again.
- Live settings show named options instead of numbers for Movement, Palette and Response focus.
- Improved colour-editor layout, MIDI controls, mini-player behaviour and Community spacing.

## Smoother everyday use
- Faster video exports with the same quality settings. Cancellation also stops the background export work.
- Notification clicks open the relevant screen or menu; the close button dismisses them.
- Recording setup and Stage Designer close when you click outside them.
- A new **What's new** window shows short highlights after an installed update, once per version.

## Downloads
- **Windows:** `Visualiser-Studio-Setup-1.3.2.exe`
- **Mac · Apple Silicon:** `Visualiser-Studio-1.3.2-mac-arm64.dmg`
- **Mac · Intel:** `Visualiser-Studio-1.3.2-mac-x64.dmg`
- ZIP versions are also available for Mac.

Windows automatic updates use only the Windows installer. Mac updates remain manual, with separate Apple Silicon and Intel downloads. Mac requires macOS 14.2 or newer. These Mac builds are ad-hoc signed and are not Apple-notarized; first launch may require **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.

The companion website is uploaded separately. New-style sharing, richer listening cards and creator broadcasts require their accompanying Community database migrations; existing activity sharing retains its text fallback until those are applied.
