# Visualiser Studio v1.2.8

## Bring your player back

Hiding the Live fullscreen player now leaves a **Show player** button in the fullscreen toolbar. Move your mouse to reveal the toolbar, then click the button to bring the player back without leaving fullscreen.

- Restores your previous cover-only or always-expanded player mode.
- Remembers the restored visibility setting.
- Keeps the recovery button out of the way when the player is already visible.

## Updating

Installed v1.2.7 users can get this release through **Settings → Updates → Check for updates**. It is published in the new public releases repository.

If you are still on v1.2.5 or v1.2.6, update to the v1.2.7 bridge first, then check again for v1.2.8.

For a manual installation, download **Visualiser-Studio-Setup-1.2.8.exe**. The blockmap and latest.yml are used by the updater.
